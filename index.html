Safety & Operational Notes
  • Always verify air assist pressure before running balsa or thin aircraft plywood.
  • Ensure exhaust suction fan is powered on to clear smoke and soot deposits.
  • Balsa density varies; increase power by 5% if material is unusually dense.
  • Clean focus lens with optics cleaner every 4 hours of active operation.
